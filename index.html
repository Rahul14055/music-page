Behind tyzen01

Independent Music Creator • Lyricist • Hybrid Producer

I’m Rahul R., an independent music creator and the artist behind tyzen01. Every track begins with real human intuition — writing my own lyrics, shaping evocative melodies, and bringing musical ideas to life by blending authentic artistry with AI-powered production.

Sounds & Stories
